Market Duel — sign-in test

Version 2.0 test page. This page only tests EVE's sign-in and citadel markets; it is not Market Duel itself.

1. Sign in

Not signed in.

You sign in on CCP's own site (login.eveonline.com), not here. Market Duel never sees your password and can only read market prices, citadel names, and search for citadels by name.

2. Find citadels with a public market

Asks EVE for the list of player-owned stations that have a public market, then looks up their names and systems (each name is one request; names are remembered in this browser).

2b. Search citadels by name

Finds citadels your character can use, including ones missing from the public list. Every citadel's name starts with its system's name, so a system name finds that system's citadels.

3. Read a citadel's market

Pick a citadel above, then read its market. Your character must be able to dock there.

Log

If something fails, copy the log and paste it into our chat. It never contains your sign-in key.
